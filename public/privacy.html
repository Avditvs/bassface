Bassface

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2025-01-01

Bassface is an unofficial client for the SoundCloud API. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SoundCloud. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your browser, and what we never do with your data.

1. Who we are

Bassface is operated by Louis Germain. For any question or request about this policy or your data, contact contact@louis-germain.fr.

2. What is stored on your device

Bassface has no account system and no server-side database. Everything it remembers is kept in your own browser and never sent to us.

localStorage

  • bassface-theme — your dark/light theme choice.
  • playlist_updater.config — the token proxy URL / redirect URI (and, only in local development, credentials you entered yourself).
  • playlist_updater.tokens — the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by SoundCloud.
  • playlist_updater.user — the signed-in SoundCloud profile returned by the API.
  • playlist_updater.chromas — the per-track musical key estimated on your device.
  • playlist_updater.bpms — the per-track tempo estimated on your device.
  • pu.organize.selected — the Reorganize sidebar filter you picked.
  • playlist_updater.discover — whether you already saw the first-use tour.

sessionStorage (cleared when the tab closes)

  • playlist_updater.code_verifier, playlist_updater.state — short-lived OAuth 2.1 + PKCE values used during sign-in.
  • pu.organize.playlist_art, pu.organize.playlist_stats — session-scoped caches of playlist artwork and aggregate stats. They are cleared when the tab closes, in line with the SoundCloud API Terms of Use.

The key and tempo analyses are numeric results computed on your device from audio you asked the app to play; they are not copies of any SoundCloud content and are kept only so an analysis never has to run twice.

3. What leaves your browser

  • OAuth token exchanges go through a small Cloudflare Worker (the token proxy), which injects the SoundCloud Client Secret server-side and does not log tokens or secrets.
  • API calls go directly from your browser to api.soundcloud.com, authenticated with your own bearer token.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate, cookie-free audience measurement: no cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, and no personal data collected.

4. SoundCloud's role

SoundCloud is an independent controller of the data you access through its API and of your SoundCloud account. Your use of SoundCloud is also governed by SoundCloud's own privacy policy and terms of use.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your data; we do not use advertising or tracking technology; we do not collect other users' names, emails or credentials; and we do not use SoundCloud content to train AI models.

6. Retention and your rights

Data stays in your browser until you sign out or clear site data. Sign out removes the stored tokens and profile immediately; clearing your browser's site data removes everything. Because we hold no copy of your data, requests to access or erase it are handled directly on your device, but you can contact us with any question at the address above.

7. Children and changes

Bassface is not directed at children and requires a SoundCloud account, which has its own age requirements. We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
